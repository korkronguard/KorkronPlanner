# Changelog

## 0.27.1

- **The Stats tab is easier to read.** It now has a section for each of your two numbers, "Staying alive" (your effective health)
  and "Damage", each with your own total and only the stats that count for it. Every stat shows how much of it you have (a
  rating with the chance it gives you, a weapon with its damage a second), what 1 more point adds, and what that's worth in
  plain words: "0.5 Strength" instead of a bare number. Hover a heading to see what its number means, or a stat for the full
  sentence.

## 0.27.0

- **New: the Stats tab.** What 1 more point of each stat adds for you, in damage or healing a second and in effective HP, with
  each stat compared with your main one (other tools call these stat weights). For Warriors the numbers come from your own
  character sheet, talents and weapons, against the enemy you choose: a normal one of your level, an elite, or an elite 3 levels
  up. Where a cap is in reach the tab says how many points are left, like "47 more, then nothing" for the hit you still need;
  hover a stat for the details. The other classes see the planner's numbers for their build with its best gear at their level,
  until their class is added.
- **Warriors: item tooltips and bag arrows now judge gear by your own numbers,** not by the planner's best gear for your level.
  An item's worth follows what you wear, your talents, your stance and whether you're tanking, and an upgrade's % is a share of
  your own damage. The Formulas tab's "Worth" lines use the same numbers.
- **Dual wielding: hit now counts for your white swings past the ability cap.** The planner said hit keeps helping them up to 24%
  (28% against a raid boss), but its math stopped at 5% (9%). Real combat logs agree with the old rule behind it: dual wielders
  missed 21.5% of their white swings, players with one weapon 4%. Nobody below the cap gets a different number; Subtlety's Solo
  build now takes a point of Precision for it.
- **The November 9 hotfixes,** with 297 more items, most of them level 50 to 60 world drops. Natural Instinct now counts for
  Lacerate and Pounce.
- **More real numbers from the guild:** Warrior base stats and crit from Agility to level 35, Paladin to 32, Priest to 23, Druid to
  36, Shaman to 40, Hunter to 34, and Hunter pets to level 33.
- **A few Solo builds move a point or two:** Protection Warriors take Improved Rend for 3 of their 5 points of Deflection (from
  level 46 on), Restoration Druids move a point from Genesis to Vengeance, Restoration Shamans one from Ancestral Knowledge to
  Elemental Weapons, and Destruction's point of Improved Corruption goes to Molten Skin. Fourteen other builds take the same
  talents in a slightly different order, most of them from level 45 on.
- **The Formulas tab** now says which rules real fights have confirmed: white-hit damage, an enemy's 5% chance to miss you, and
  the dual-wield penalty.
- **The DPS tab's elite columns now count your own Sunder Armor,** 5 from the start and then kept up: Warriors read about 17% higher
  there. A real combat log shows each stack taking 180 armor off at level 31, so a long fight rewards stacking it early. The normal
  column doesn't count it: a normal mob is usually close to dying by the time 5 are up, and that rage does as much elsewhere.
- **Dungeon healers:** the talent orders now use the mana of a real dungeon run: short pulls, and no drinking between them, as a real
  Restoration Druid's run in Gnomeregan shows. Bloom counts too, for what's left of it after overhealing. Restoration Druids take Bloom
  at 20 instead of 28, Holy Priests start with Holy Specialization, and Restoration Shamans with Tidal Focus. Level 60 numbers don't
  change.

## 0.26.0

- **Enhancement Shamans: Stormstrike now counts as soon as you take it.** The planner had it locked until level 40, the level in the
  game's files, but the talent gives it straight away: a level 36 Shaman used it 210 times in a real dungeon. Enhancement's Solo,
  Dungeon and PvP orders now take it at level 25 to 27 instead of 34 to 38, and Restoration's Solo at 30 instead of 40. The Priority
  tab and gear lists count it from then on.
- **Counterattack and Vampiric Embrace** show from level 25, for the same reason.
- **The DPS tab:** the small print now says what a real dungeon measured, and which column may still read a little high. The top line
  only mentions shield talents when you're holding a shield.
- **The latest server hotfixes,** with 56 more items, and more real numbers from the guild: Warrior, Druid and Shaman base stats at
  higher levels.
- **Some talent orders and gear lists changed.** No build changes its talents, but some take them at different levels.

## 0.25.0

- **New: the DPS tab (Warriors so far).** Your damage a second, worked out in game from your own character sheet, talents and
  weapons, in each stance you know, against a normal mob of your level, an elite of your level and an elite 3 levels up. Say
  whether you're tanking it or someone else is, and hover a number for what it's made of. Other classes will come one at a time.
- **Warrior numbers now follow the game.** From a real combat log: your rage comes from your weapon's swing time, not how hard
  you hit, so every weapon gives the same rage a second, and a crit gives double. Being hit gives rage in every stance. Thunder
  Clap and Rend gain from attack power, though their tooltips don't say so, and armor reduces Thunder Clap. Revenge hits harder
  than the planner thought. Most Warrior builds rise up to 7%; raid Fury and Arms fall about 10% at 60, because they counted on
  big hits for rage. That rule hasn't been seen at level 60 yet, so those two are the planner's best reading until a level 60
  Warrior's log confirms it.
- **Protection's Priority list:** Slam is on it now, for when you're alone, and Revenge comes before Shield Slam: it does the most
  damage for its rage.
- **Enemies as they really are.** Armor, health and how hard mobs hit now come from the same log. Physical classes do more while
  leveling, as enemy armor was set too high.
- **Your Hunter pet's Bite now counts, so keep it on autocast and keep it trained.** It adds a hit every 10 seconds, using the
  game's own numbers and a combat log's: a well-fed pet's Bite does 25% more. A pet keeps the rank it was taught, so a low-rank Bite
  stays weak until you teach it a new one. A pet with Claw on autocast too bites less often, as Claw spends its focus. Hunters with a
  pet rise a few percent at 60. Marksmanship's raid build goes without a pet (Lone Wolf), so it stays the same.
- **The latest server hotfixes,** with 140 more items, and more real numbers from the guild: Paladin, Druid, Hunter and Shaman base
  stats at higher levels.
- **Some talent orders and gear lists changed.** No build changes its talents, but some take them at different levels. The
  Formulas tab's rage lines follow the new rule.

## 0.24.0

- **Hunter pets now count.** A Hunter's numbers add your pet's melee, measured from a guild Hunter's pet and a combat log.
  Beast Mastery rises by about a third at 60 and Survival by about 15%. Marksmanship's raid build goes without a pet
  (Lone Wolf), so it stays the same. Above level 29 the pet's numbers are estimates until a higher level Hunter reports in.
- **Pet talents have real values.** Unleashed Fury, Ferocity, Frenzy, Bestial Wrath and Focused Fire show what they add.
  A pet doesn't crit without Ferocity, and Frenzy needs its crits. Some Hunter talent orders and gear lists changed; no
  build changed its talents.
- **The Formulas tab has a "Your pet" section** for Hunters, with what each pet family's damage bonus means: the numbers
  count a family with no bonus, so a Cat or Raptor does 10% better and a Boar or Turtle 10% worse.

## 0.23.0

- **Your build in the game's talent window.** Press **N**: the talent to take next glows gold, the other points you
  can spend now are numbered in order (up to 5), and every talent of your build shows your points and the build's
  (a green tick when it's done). Talents outside the build are left alone, and you can still click anything: the marks
  follow your clicks before you press Apply. Hovering a talent adds what the build says about it, why the planner
  picks it and the talents it rates nearly as good. No more switching between **/kp** and the talent window.
- **A Kor'kron bar along the bottom of the talent window:** choose your build there (Solo, Dungeon or PvP for each
  spec, or **No build** to keep the window plain), see the next talent, and open **/kp** with the **Planner** button.
- **Mana regen corrected.** Spirit gives Spirit ÷ 4 mana a second, but each point above 50 Spirit counts half (read
  from the guild's own characters, seen up to 91 Spirit). The Formulas tab, item tooltips and gear lists use it. Mana
  users' gear lists moved, Mages and Hunters most, and Fire and Arcane Mages run lower at 60 because they run shorter
  on mana. Some talent orders changed; no build changed its talents.
- **Real base stats for Shamans, Hunters and Druids,** from the guild's first Shaman and Hunter and a Tauren Druid.
  They're lower than the old estimates, by up to 13 Stamina at level 34.
- **The latest server hotfixes,** with their new items.
- **Fixed: gear with a random bonus.** Wearing the "of the Bear" version of an item, its "of the Monkey" version said
  "You wear this" and got no upgrade check or bag arrow. Different bonuses are now compared properly.

## 0.22.1

- **Better leveling orders for every class that uses mana.** The planner now works out more carefully how hard to
  spend your mana while leveling (a little more mana could make it pick a costly spell that cost more in drinking than
  it saved). Warlocks now Life Tap and eat it back instead of drinking for everything. Many Solo and PvP builds take
  their talents in a slightly different order; none levels slower than before.
- **Five Solo builds changed a few points:**
  - Frost, Fire and Arcane Mages take fewer points in Precision: against monsters of your level, more than 2 ranks of
    hit does almost nothing. The points go to Arcane Concentration (Frost), Arcane Blast (Fire), and Improved Fire
    Blast and Master of Elements (Arcane).
  - Demonology Warlocks move two points from Malevolence into Bane (faster Shadow Bolts).
  - Elemental Shamans move one Concussion point into Tidal Focus (more hit).
  - If you follow one of these, `/kp` shows the new order; the points you've already spent still count.

## 0.22.0

- **Tanks are measured on threat.** The planner now works out threat per second from your damage and the game's
  own threat bonuses, multiplied together (the original game's rule): Defensive Stance +80% and Defiance +20%,
  Righteous Fury +33% (and +90% more on Holy damage), Bear Form +115%, Iron Creed on Holy Strike, Holy Shield's damage.
  Sunder Armor's threat counts too. Tank Dungeon builds now weigh survival and threat (they used damage as a stand-in),
  and hovering a threat talent's point shows what it adds. The extra threat that Shield Slam, Revenge, Heroic Strike,
  Lacerate and Primal Bite add is worked out by the game's server and isn't counted.
  - Warrior Protection's Dungeon build takes Master of Defense a little earlier (more rage for Sunder Armor).
  - Protection Paladins: Holy Shield's damage on blocks now counts, and so does Shield Specialization's mana from
    blocks.
- **Hover the gear you're wearing** to see what it gives you for your build: its worth in % (like an upgrade's), then
  stat by stat, with what those points do on your character sheet (Strength into attack power, Agility into crit,
  dodge and armor...). Until now the tooltip said nothing on gear you wear.
- **EllesmereUI's bags show the green upgrade arrow** (top right of the slot), as the game's own bags and Baganator do.
- **Item data from the game's November 7 update:** about 430 more items the upgrade tooltips, the bag arrow and the
  Gear tab know (new dungeon loot among them), and the lower stats Adaptive Combat Assistant, Weakness Analyzer and
  Serenity Field got that day.

## 0.21.0

- **Solo, Dungeon and PvP builds for every class.** In **/kp**, your class's builds are now grouped by spec, each with
  three paths: **Solo** (questing alone), **Dungeon** (ready for groups while you level, then dungeons and raids at 60)
  and **PvP** (world PvP while you level, battlegrounds at 60). Druids have two Feral builds on Solo and Dungeon (Cat and
  Bear).
  - **Dungeon** paths are measured in dungeon pulls at each level (the gear you'd have then, no resting counted), with
    their own Priority and Gear lists from level 10. Tanks take their threat talents as soon as they can (Paladin:
    Holy Shield, Iron Creed; Warrior: Defiance, Improved Sunder Armor; Druid: Feral Instinct, Lacerate). The Warrior's
    separate "Protection: leveling (dungeons)" build is now Protection's Dungeon path: if you followed it, you follow
    that now.
  - **PvP** builds, one per spec, for example: Paladin Retribution (Repentance, a quicker Hammer of Justice, Eye for an
    Eye), Warrior Arms (Improved Hamstring, Piercing Howl), Priest Shadow (Blackout, Silence, Silent Resolve), Rogue
    Subtlety (Preparation, Elusiveness, Improved Sprint), Mage Frost (Frostbite, Improved Frost Nova, Ice Block),
    Hunter Survival (Improved Wing Clip, Counterattack, Deterrence), Druid Feral (Brutal Impact, Feral Charge), Shaman
    Elemental (Earthbound, Eye of the Storm), Warlock Demonology (Soul Link, Fel Domination). They take the crowd
    control, stuns and utility a PvE build skips. The planner can't measure a fight against a player, so these talents
    are picked from class knowledge; Beast Mastery and Marksmanship Hunters and Elemental Shamans follow the PvP builds
    Icy Veins publishes for WoW: Forever. Each build says which. The Gear tab ranks their gear with the planner's
    questing numbers.
- **Builds checked by the planner's own math:** a new search moves points wherever the math says the build gets
  better, and 22 Solo and Dungeon builds changed. For example: Arms Solo takes Improved Slam; Frost and Fire Mages take
  Arcane Blast (on Forever its stacks add spell damage to everything you cast); the Protection Paladin takes Conviction;
  Combat Rogue Solo trades Hack and Slash for Improved Poisons; Restoration Shaman Solo takes Shamanistic Focus. Hover a
  build's name to see how many of its 51 points the math measures. The rest are picked by judgment (utility and crowd
  control the math can't put a number on yet), and the planner website names them.
- **Warriors in dungeons:** a damage-dealing Warrior's Enrage no longer counts there (the tank is the one being hit).
- **Make a build yours:**
  - Hover a point to see the talents the planner rates as good or nearly as good there.
  - "Your choice" points: a point or a few in every build can go either way (the window lists them; Warlocks can take
    a stronger Succubus for faster kills). Taking the other option counts as following the build.
  - Points you spend outside the build are your own picks, no longer marked red: the build drops its last points to
    make room, and the Talents tab shows your picks in blue.
- **Hunters and Warlocks: your pet's stats are shared too** (its kind, level, stats, health, mana, armor, attack
  power, damage, attack speed and happiness; not its name). The game's files don't say how hard pets hit on Forever, so today
  the planner counts no pet damage at all. Your pet's numbers let it value Beast Mastery and Demonology talents from
  real numbers. You'll see the sharing message once more because of this; **/kp share off** still stops all sharing.

## 0.19.0

- **Leveling fights now end when the mob dies.** A mob of your level lives about 8 to 22 seconds, so damage over time
  (Shadow Word: Pain, Corruption, Rend, Serpent Sting, Moonfire...) only counts the ticks that land before it dies,
  and long cooldowns (Death Wish, racials) count as coming back while you eat and drink. Leveling builds that lean on
  damage over time come out slower, and 12 leveling talent orders changed (Discipline, Shadow and Holy Priest,
  Elemental and Enhancement, Protection Warrior, Restoration and Balance Druid, all three Warlocks). Affliction keeps
  Bane of Agony while leveling: Bane of Doom's damage comes after a minute. Level 60 builds are unchanged.
- **Warriors:** Overpower, Slam, Rend and Thunder Clap are now part of the damage math. Arms does about 25% more than
  the planner thought, and the Arms, Arms leveling and both Protection leveling orders changed. Fury's Priority list
  now has Slam, with the white damage its cast costs.
- **Warlocks:** one Bane at a time, Conflagrate uses up your Immolate, Incinerate's bonus on an Immolated target, and
  Shadow and Flame's bonus only when you cast Conflagrate and Shadowburn. Affliction and Demonology estimates drop by
  about a tenth; builds and gear follow.
- **Arcane Mages:** Arcane Blast's stacks are in the math; the Priority list says how many Blasts per 18 seconds pay off.
- **Priority tab:** Shadow Priests (Mind Blast when you have mana to spare, wand when you're out), Balance Druids,
  Rogues (Slice and Dice kept up, poisons counted) and Enhancement Shamans (Searing Totem; Magma Totem and Fire Nova
  for packs) get lists that fit their talents.
- **Fixes:** Wand Specialization, Ranged Weapon Specialization and Furious Precision only count with their weapon;
  ranged attack power was counted twice; melee classes no longer get Auto Shot damage.
- **Real numbers from the guild:** Warrior and Paladin base stats are now measured up to level 22, Priests to 12
  (thanks to everyone sharing). Paladin Spirit above level 19 was a little low and is fixed.
- Keep WowUp's auto-update on to get new versions without checking.

  spell numbers against the game once per patch: your class's talent tooltips are compared right in your game (only a

## 0.13.0

- **New: a rotation helper (Warriors first).** Type **/kp helper**, or tick "Rotation helper" in the `/kp` window: a
  small bar shows what to press now (the big icon) and the next two, each with the game's own cooldown swirl. Hover an
  icon to see why it's there. It follows your suggested build at your level, shows in combat or when you target an
  enemy (Charge, Bloodrage and your shouts come up before the pull), and against elites and bosses adds your big
  cooldowns. Drag it where you like; **/kp helper lock** keeps it there. WoW: Forever hides your rage, your buff timers
  and your target's health from addons during a fight, so the helper keeps its own notes of what you cast and never
  suggests Heroic Strike: use that yourself when you have rage to spare. Other classes will follow.
- **Better Warrior priorities** (the Priority tab and the helper): attacks are ordered by how much damage each point of
  rage buys, so Fury uses Whirlwind before Bloodthirst and Arms puts Overpower and Slam ahead of Mortal Strike. In the
  Execute phase, attacks that still beat Execute stay ahead of it. Protection opens with Shield Block (it doesn't
  use the global cooldown) and adds Thunder Clap and Rend; every Warrior gets Charge and Bloodrage before the pull and
  Victory Rush while leveling.
- **The Formulas tab explains every number:** under each formula and rule, what each number is and where it comes
  from: the game's files, the original game's rules, checked against real Forever characters, Forever's patch notes,
  or the planner's estimate. Rage now shows its conversion value and the rage your own average hit gives.
- **Data straight from the game:** the planner now reads its data from the WoW: Forever game itself, with the
  server's latest changes: about 2,100 more items in the Gear tab and the upgrade tooltips.

## 0.12.0

- **Better builds:** every suggested build was compared with the popular WoW: Forever guides (Icy Veins) and checked
  with the planner's math; 38 of the 56 builds now take better talents. A few: Destruction sacrifices its Imp for +15%
  Shadow damage, Affliction takes Suppression and Agonizing Flames, Elemental keeps a longer Flame Shock up, Warriors

- **A bigger `/kp` window:** wider tabs, bigger talent icons, the next 14 talent points instead of 10, and room for

## 0.10.0
